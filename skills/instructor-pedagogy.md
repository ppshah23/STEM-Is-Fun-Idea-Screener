# Instructor Pedagogy

This is shared context, not an agentic step of its own — it's passed into
`pipeline.steps.analyze_course` (and can be referenced by other steps) so
every skill operates under the same teaching philosophy.

## STEM is FUN core pedagogy principles

- **Productive struggle first.** Students should be given room to try,
  fail, and retry before an instructor intervenes. Don't design activities
  that remove all friction — friction is where the learning happens.
- **Hands-on before abstract.** Introduce a concept through a physical
  build or experiment before (or instead of) a lecture-style explanation.
- **Curiosity-driven framing.** Activities should connect to a question a
  student would actually want answered, not just "because it's on the
  curriculum."
- **Consistency across instructors.** Different instructors may have
  different personal styles, but the core principles above are
  non-negotiable across every course this pipeline produces.
